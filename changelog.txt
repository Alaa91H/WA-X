[COMPATIBILITY]
* Added WhatsApp Messenger 2.26.38.xx and 2.26.39.xx support.
* Added WhatsApp Business 2.26.38.xx support.
* Added Android 17 / API 37 support to the downgrade compatibility hook.

[SECURITY]
* Restricted bridge Binder access to WaEnhancer, WhatsApp, and WhatsApp Business UIDs.
* Restricted remote preference reads to trusted WaEnhancer/WhatsApp callers.
* Protected internal control broadcasts with a signature-level permission.
* Added authentication for Tasker MESSAGE_SENT intents and scoped outgoing Tasker broadcasts.

[STABILITY]
* Completed the custom SharedPreferences implementation (string sets, clear, apply, commit).
* Fixed HookProvider initialization.
* Fixed Tasker event delivery that was previously disabled unconditionally.
* Release workflow now builds signed release APKs and fails when signing secrets are missing.
